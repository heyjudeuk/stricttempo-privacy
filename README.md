# StrictTempo Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Developer:** Jude Sudbury

## Overview
StrictTempo ("the App") is a music player for ballroom, Latin, and social dancers on iOS and watchOS. Your privacy is important to us: **StrictTempo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## Data Collection & Storage
- **No Personal Information Collected:** The App does not require an account, registration, or login, and does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
- **Local Audio & Library Data:** All music files, playlists, cue points, dance style settings, and tempo configurations you create or import are stored locally on your device (and synced directly between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch via Apple's local WatchConnectivity framework). None of this data ever leaves your devices or is transmitted to external servers.

## In-App Purchases (Tip Jar)
- Optional tips made through the in-app Tip Jar are processed securely and directly by Apple via StoreKit and the App Store.
- The Developer does not receive or have access to your payment card details or billing information.

## Third-Party Analytics & Advertising
- StrictTempo contains **no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking scripts, and no advertisements**.

## Children's Privacy
- Because the App does not collect any information from any user of any age, it complies fully with children's privacy regulations including COPPA and GDPR.

## Changes to This Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated in future versions of the App, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
