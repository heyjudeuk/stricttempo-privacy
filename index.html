StrictTempo for iOS & watchOS

Privacy Policy & Support

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Developer: Jude Sudbury

1. Overview

StrictTempo is a practice and teaching music player for ballroom, Latin, and social dancers on iPhone and Apple Watch. We respect your privacy: StrictTempo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

  • No Personal Data Collected: StrictTempo does not require an account or login and does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or usage diagnostics.
  • 100% On-Device Library: All audio files, playlists, dance style settings, cue/exit points, and BPM configurations remain stored locally on your device. Communication between your iPhone and Apple Watch occurs directly between your paired devices using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework.

3. In-App Purchases (Tip Jar)

Optional tips made through the in-app Tip Jar are processed directly by Apple using StoreKit. We never receive or store your payment or billing details.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

StrictTempo contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no user tracking of any kind.
